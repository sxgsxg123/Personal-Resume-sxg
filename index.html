About

I am a PhD student in the School of Cyber Science and Engineering at Wuhan University. I previously completed a master’s degree at Huazhong University of Science and Technology and a bachelor’s degree at Qingdao University.

My work brings together questions in cryptographic security and complex-variable chaotic systems, including hardware-oriented applications.
